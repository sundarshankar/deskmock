# Interview Prep Resources — curated by dimension

A vetted set of open-source GitHub resources for interview prep, organized by the dimensions a
**platform / cloud / infrastructure leader** (Director / VP / Head) faces. DeskMock + JobHelm use
this to auto-link the right prep to a specific JD (by role level + tech stack) and to seed question
sets. ⭐ = best fit for senior platform-leadership roles.

## 🎯 Leadership & Engineering Management (Director / VP / Head)
_Org design, strategy, hiring, stakeholder management, cross-functional influence._
- ⭐ [kaushikb9/em-interviews](https://github.com/kaushikb9/em-interviews) — interview questions for **EM / Director / VP Eng** roles (your level)
- ⭐ [engineering-management/awesome-engineering-management](https://github.com/engineering-management/awesome-engineering-management) — EM & leadership practice (already a reference submodule)
- [jakubsvobodacz/Awesome-Engineering-Manager](https://github.com/jakubsvobodacz/Awesome-Engineering-Manager) — EM resources + STAR-format practice

- [charlax/engineering-management](https://github.com/charlax/engineering-management) — EM resources (reference submodule)

## How this maps to a JD (the plan)
For a given role, DeskMock/JobHelm will:
1. **Classify the JD** — level (Director/VP), domain (platform/cloud/SRE), and the named stack (K8s, Terraform, Tetrate, DORA, FinOps…).
2. **Link the matching dimensions above** (always leadership + behavioral for Director/VP; technical + system-design weighted by the JD).
3. **Seed a question set** per dimension (via the existing DeskMock question generator), grounded in your CV.
4. **Scan recent articles** on the JD's key stack (current developments) to complement the fundamentals — so you sound current, not just well-read.

_Add more per dimension over time — PRs welcome._
