## Your data and your API key
The tools are **local-first**: your data stays on your machine, there are no servers operated by this
project, and there is no telemetry. However, any feature that calls a language model **sends the relevant
text — for example your CV and the job description — to the AI provider you configure** (such as
OpenRouter, Anthropic, or OpenAI). That data is handled under **that provider's** terms of service and
privacy policy. Choose a provider you trust, and don't paste anything you're not comfortable sending to it.
